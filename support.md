# SplitIt Support

**Questions? Issues? We're here to help.**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I enter a bill amount?**
Tap the dollar field at the top and type your total. The split updates instantly as you type.

**How do I change the tip percentage?**
Tap one of the preset buttons (10%, 15%, 18%, 20%, 25%) or drag the slider to any custom amount.

**What does "Round Up" do?**
When enabled, each person's share is rounded up to the nearest $0.25 — useful for cash payments so no one has to deal with odd cents.

**How many people can I split between?**
Between 1 and 30 people using the + and − buttons.

**Does SplitIt save my bills?**
No — SplitIt is intentionally simple. Each session starts fresh. No data is stored or synced anywhere.

**Does SplitIt require an internet connection?**
No. SplitIt works completely offline. No account, no login, no internet needed.

**Does SplitIt cost anything?**
SplitIt is free with no ads and no in-app purchases.
